# Source expansion research

These are the findings from October 2026. They record what a reviewer actually obtained at the time. Check the current distribution and terms again before publishing a new inventory. The [source register](../../../docs/source-apis/index.md) has the 44 published inventories, counts and attribution. This file covers prospects outside that register and the six withheld locations.

## What counts as an inventory

Municipal APIs, spreadsheets and file downloads can all supply tree records. A basemap, public viewer or register of permits can identify a research lead. Publication requires an identifiable distribution, permission covering the intended use, a complete or explicitly bounded acquisition, source-scoped IDs, verified axes and coordinate system, and clear meanings for dates, dimensions and unit of record. Retain rows with missing positions in search. Keep original-language names. Record the limits of groups, avenues, stumps, planting sites and historic surveys.

There was no customer survey, traffic forecast, revenue study or commercial market-size estimate. The market research in this project concerned public data availability, coverage and hosting options.

## Europe

The October sweep covered the UK, Spain, Italy, Switzerland, France, Portugal, Germany, Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg and Poland. Vienna supplied the first Austrian source. Published city notes distinguish points from parks, municipal management from geographical coverage, and data licences from the code licence. Some published entries are metropolitan, cantonal, borough or national heritage collections. ### Mainland China

Guangzhou–Haizhu had the strongest individual-record evidence. The portal advertised 70 location rows and 1,604 separate attribute rows. Ten samples from each feed were checked. Codes joined information across the two tables. Numeric longitude and latitude looked plausible. The complete downloads required login and the coordinate system remained unspecified. The later city specification mentions CGCS2000 but took effect after the recorded dataset update. Confirm the datum with the publisher. The platform agreement listed attribution, application filing, restrictions on selling source data and third-party rights. Clarify how filing applies to a public normalized release. Verify the counts, join coverage, duplicates and coordinates using authorized files. [Location catalogue](https://gddata.gd.gov.cn/opdata/detail?id=3070220105032_000002) · [Attribute catalogue](https://gddata.gd.gov.cn/opdata/detail?id=3070220105032_000003) · [City standard](https://scjgj.gz.gov.cn/attachment/7/7834/7834262/10321577.pdf) · [Agreement](https://gddata.gd.gov.cn/agreement)

## Copernicus

The 2021 Street Tree Layer represents mapped patches and rows in selected Urban Atlas functional urban areas. The product’s minimum mapped area is 500 m². An individual species, inspection date or municipal tag cannot be inferred from a polygon. The product page and 2021 manual differed over the minimum width rule. Keep the product version visible. Reference files were offered in EPSG:3035; the browser pilot uses an online image overlay. The initial feasibility comparison found about 5.82 MB for Vienna and 828 MB for the European package. Download size does not predict browser memory. Keep canopy reference time, tile availability and licensing independent of municipal search and offline packs.
